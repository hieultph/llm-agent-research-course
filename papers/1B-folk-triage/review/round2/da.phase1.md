## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by methodology): From an adversarial stance, this dimension asks whether the study design, data handling and statistical reporting would survive a hostile replication attempt. I would press on whether the reported procedure is complete enough for someone else to rerun it and obtain the same numbers, and whether the reported statistics actually license the comparisons drawn. I do not score this dimension, but challenges I raise about design choices may feed into it.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by domain): This dimension asks whether domain claims match current evidence and whether prior work and technical terminology are represented faithfully. Adversarially, the question is whether any claim about the domain, or about what earlier studies found, would collapse when checked against the cited source or the field's accepted knowledge. I do not score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by da): This is my dimension. It asks whether the central thesis holds together: whether each conclusion follows from the evidence presented, whether the chain from question to design to result to claim has gaps, and whether fallacies, overgeneralisation, selective use of evidence, or unaddressed alternative explanations undermine the core argument. My job is to find the weakest link in that chain and test whether the thesis survives its strongest counter-argument.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by perspective): This dimension asks whether readers from adjacent fields can follow the framing and definitions, and whether claims that cross disciplinary lines are actually substantiated. Adversarially, I would look for implications asserted for neighbouring fields or stakeholders without support. I do not score it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by eic): This dimension covers organisation, clarity, figure and table quality, and venue conventions. Adversarially, the concern is whether presentation choices obscure or overstate what the evidence shows. I do not score it.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by eic): This dimension asks whether the work fits its configured venue and contributes something original and significant to that readership. Adversarially, the question is the "so what?" test: whether the contribution would still matter once its strongest framing is stripped away. I do not score it, and with no venue binding I make no venue-alignment claim.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether every headline claim traces to specific reported evidence; whether the stated research question, design, results and conclusions form an unbroken chain; whether alternative explanations for the main findings are raised and addressed; whether conclusions stay within the scope of the sample, models and conditions actually tested; whether evidence is selected or framed to favour the thesis; and whether the strongest counter-argument to the thesis is acknowledged.
what_triggers_block: A central conclusion rests on a logical gap, an unaddressed plausible alternative explanation, or a generalisation beyond the tested conditions, such that the thesis as stated is not supported until the argument is repaired or the claim is narrowed.
what_triggers_warn: Secondary claims overreach the evidence, some alternative explanations or limitations are under-discussed, or wording in places implies more certainty or generality than the results support, while the core thesis itself still follows from the evidence.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis is contradicted by the paper's own reported evidence, or depends on a circular or fallacious inference that cannot be fixed by narrowing claims, so no revision short of a different argument or study would make it coherent.
