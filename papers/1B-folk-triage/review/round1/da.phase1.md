## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by methodology): From an adversarial stance, this dimension asks whether the study design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility support could survive a hostile replication attempt. I would probe whether design choices leave room for confounds, whether reported effects could be artefacts of sampling, scoring or chance, and whether another team could rebuild the pipeline from what is disclosed. This is not my dimension to score; I may still raise challenges that bear on it.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by domain): This dimension asks whether the claims hold against current domain evidence, whether prior work is represented fairly rather than strawmanned or selectively cited, and whether domain terminology and reported results are factually correct. Adversarially, I would look for places where a domain expert could show a claim is outdated, overstated or mislabelled. I do not score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by da): This is my scored dimension. It asks whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether each claimed conclusion is actually licensed by the evidence presented, and whether any fallacy (causal overreach, equivocation, overgeneralisation, circularity, cherry-picked support) breaks the main line of argument. My task is to build the strongest counter-argument and test whether the thesis survives it.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by perspective): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications are understandable to readers from adjacent fields and whether any claims that cross disciplinary boundaries are substantiated rather than asserted. Adversarially, I would watch for borrowed concepts used loosely and for implications stated for stakeholders whose perspective is never examined. I do not score it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by eic): This dimension asks whether the manuscript is well organised, clearly written, uses figures and tables effectively and follows venue conventions. Adversarially, I would note where presentation hides or blurs a weak inferential step. I do not score it.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by eic): This dimension asks whether the work fits the target venue and makes an original, significant contribution for its readers. Adversarially, I would apply the "so what?" test and ask whether the contribution is incremental, already known, or smaller than claimed. I do not score it.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated thesis, research questions, results and conclusions form one consistent chain; whether every headline claim traces to specific reported evidence; whether causal or mechanistic language is matched by a design that can support it; whether alternative explanations are named and ruled out; and whether generalisations stay within the scope of the evaluated conditions.
what_triggers_block: A headline conclusion (title, abstract or contribution statement) rests on an inferential step the evidence does not support, such as causal attribution from a design that cannot separate the proposed cause from a plausible confound, or a claim that contradicts the paper's own reported results, and fixing it would require reframing the central claim or adding new analysis.
what_triggers_warn: The main thesis holds, but secondary claims overreach, hedging is inconsistent between sections, a credible alternative explanation is left unaddressed, or generalisation beyond the tested conditions is asserted without qualification; each can be repaired by rewording, scoping or adding a limitation.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is internally contradicted or entirely unsupported, for example the reported evidence points the opposite way from the stated conclusion or the argument depends on circular reasoning, so that no revision short of a different paper could rescue the core claim.
