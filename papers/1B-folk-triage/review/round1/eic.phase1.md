## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): From an oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study is built and reported so that a peer reviewer in the field could trust and, in principle, repeat it: a defensible design, careful handling of the data, honest statistical reporting, and enough procedural detail and artefacts for reproduction. I do not score it, but I will treat a methodology-seat block here as decisive for the panel outcome.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): This dimension checks that what the manuscript asserts matches the current state of evidence in its domain, that earlier work is described fairly and correctly, and that domain terminology and reported results are free of factual error. It is outside my seat; I rely on the domain reviewer for it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with methodology also eligible): This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together, whether each claim is carried by the evidence offered for it, and whether any reasoning flaw undermines the main argument. It is not mine to score, though I will note when a headline framing visibly outruns the evidence because that also affects contribution.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective seat): This dimension asks whether readers from neighbouring fields can follow the framing, definitions and implications, and whether any claim that crosses disciplinary lines is properly supported. It belongs to the perspective reviewer.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by me): This dimension covers how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it explains itself, whether figures and tables do their job, and whether it follows the conventions of the configured venue, including its format and length expectations. A shortfall here leads to revision requests rather than rejection.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by me): This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs at the configured venue and offers something original and significant enough to matter to that venue's readers. Because it is mandatory, a block here forces major revision and a fatal finding forces rejection, so I must define those thresholds carefully before seeing the paper.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A logical section order that leads from motivation to findings, a title and abstract whose claims match what the results actually show, figures and tables that are legible and self-explanatory, consistent terminology, and compliance with LNCS formatting and page-length norms for the configured track.
what_triggers_block: The title or abstract states a finding that the results sections do not support, or the manuscript substantially exceeds the venue's page limit or ignores its required template such that it could not be accepted without major restructuring.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity problems, redundant or unbalanced sections, figures or tables that need better captions or labels, minor template deviations, or length that requires moderate trimming to fit the venue.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: A clearly stated contribution that is new relative to existing English-language and multilingual work in the same evaluation area, evidence that the contribution matters to an applied NLP or AI-for-health audience, and a scope that sits within the configured track.
what_triggers_block: The claimed novelty over closely related prior work is not demonstrated or is substantially overstated, or the significance for the venue's readers is asserted without support, so that the contribution must be reframed or substantiated before acceptance.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is genuine but incremental or under-positioned against prior work, or its significance and practical implications for the venue's audience are only partly articulated.
what_triggers_fatal: The work falls outside the configured venue's scope, or it essentially duplicates already published work with no identifiable original contribution.
